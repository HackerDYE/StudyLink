## Problem

UBC students looking for peers to discuss coursework or prepare for exams often face two challenges: finding students in the same courses and coordinating compatible schedules. Existing platforms such as Reddit, RedNote, and Piazza may help students connect, but availability must usually be discussed separately through repeated messages. This can delay arrangements and make suitable study groups difficult to form.

These initial observations informed our focus on course-based discovery and schedule matching. We will validate them through feedback from UBC students.

## Design Solution

UBC StudyLink helps UBC students discover and join study groups that fit their academic interests, schedules, and learning preferences. Students create profiles using a UBC email address and provide their courses, major, weekly availability, and preferred languages.

The platform supports two ways to find a study group:

1. **Personalized recommendations:** The system recommends existing study groups based on students' profile information, including shared courses, academic backgrounds, overlapping availability, and language preferences. Students choose which recommended groups they would like to join.

2. **Browse and filter:** Students can explore a study group directory and filter groups by course, meeting time, and meeting pattern, such as one-time or recurring sessions.


## Core Features

### 1. Recommended

Students can open **Recommended** from the sidebar to view suggested study groups based on their courses, availability, major, and preferred languages. Each recommendation shows the group's course, meeting schedule, available places, and reasons for the match. Students can view group details and request to join.

### 2. My Profile

Customize academic information, enrolled courses, preferred languages, meeting patterns, and weekly availability for personalized group recommendations.
