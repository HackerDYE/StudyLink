## Problem
Students looking for peers to discuss coursework or prepare for exams need both shared courses and compatible schedules. Our team identifies Reddit, RedNote and Piazza posts as existing ways to find peers, but students still need separate conversations to compare availability. This can delay arrangements and make suitable partners difficult to find. These observations come from our team discussion; supporting user research remains to be added.


## Design Solution

UBC StudyLink helps UBC students discover and join study groups that fit their academic interests, schedules, and learning preferences. Students create profiles using a UBC email address and provide their courses, major, weekly availability, and preferred languages.

The platform supports two ways to find a study group:

1. **Personalized recommendations:** The system recommends existing study groups based on students' profile information, including shared courses, academic backgrounds, overlapping availability, and language preferences. Students choose which recommended groups they would like to join.

2. **Browse and filter:** Students can explore a study group directory and filter groups by course, meeting time, and meeting pattern, such as one-time or recurring sessions.
